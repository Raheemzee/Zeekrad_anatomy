ZeekaRad Anatomy workstation, local build

Seven linked panels on real images, with every structure the installed models can segment named under the pointer in 14 languages.

What is shown, and where it comes from

  • Patient-derived, automated: the labels in panels B, D, E, F and G are model output (TotalSegmentator) on the study's own grid. No clinician has reviewed them.
  • Independent reference: panel C shows another body: a labelled CT of another subject, or cadaver cryosections. It is never registered to the patient and never follows the patient's scrolling. Selecting a structure moves the reference to the structure of the same name.
  • Names: English is canonical. Other languages come from ZeekaRad's term base or from Wikidata (CC0), as recorded per structure. Neither is clinician-reviewed. Where no source gives a name, the English name is shown and marked EN; nothing is machine-translated.

Segmentation engine

    Data and licences

    • Sample CT studies and the labelled reference CT: TotalSegmentator CT dataset v2.0.1 (Wasserthal et al.), CC BY 4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.10047292.
    • Sample MR study: AMOS22 (Ji et al.), CC BY 4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.7262581.
    • Cadaver cryosections: Visible Human Project, male. Courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Not the most current data available from NLM.
    • Rendering: three.js (MIT) and vtk.js by Kitware (BSD 3-Clause). Nothing from IMAIOS or any other commercial atlas is used.

    Limits

    • CT and MR volumes only. Projection images, ultrasound, multi-frame and time-resolved series are refused at upload with the reason.
    • The MR model names 50 structures of the trunk and the brain as one structure. Nerves, small vessel branches, ligaments and fascial spaces are not segmented by any model here.
    • A structure missing from the list is not evidence that it is absent in the patient.
